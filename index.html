What makes us different

More than just dance classes.

You want a place where your daughter grows — not just as a dancer, but as a person. A place where she feels known, encouraged, and confident in who God made her to be.

At His Image Performing Arts, we train students technically, pour into them spiritually, and build a community that feels like family. Dance should build more than skill — it should build character, confidence, and purpose.

Programs

Designed for every stage.

Classes for ages 3–18, meeting Mondays in Chapel Hill. Every level moves from recreational technique toward company opportunity.

Ages 3–7 · Littles

Creative Movement & Ballet A

From $35/month

Introduction to worship and expression through movement, ballet-based and full of imagination.

Recreational & Junior Company

Ballet and jazz technique, Scripture study, and performance at outreach and recital.
